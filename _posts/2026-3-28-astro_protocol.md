In theory, 4X should be one of my favorite genre of games. What's not to like? Building your imaginary empire, recruiting troops and sending them on your pesky neighbours seems like a great hobby to have. As I get older my desire to be a great ruler is getting stronger and stronger. However, with age comes a shortage - time. Being an emperor usually takes a lot of time and it doesn't matter if you try to be a historical one in a Civilization game, a wizard in Age of Wonders or a warmonger in Endless Space. It can take dozens of hours of game time to just finish a single match!

Astro Protocol 

Look on my works, ye mighty, and despair!
